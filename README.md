# Data Analysis & Data Science Lab (❁´◡`❁)

Welcome to my Data Analysis & Data Science workspace.

I'm an Artificial Intelligence student at IFRI-UAC, and this repository is where I explore data, practice analytical methods and develop my understanding of the data science field.

It brings together some of my academic work, personal experiments, practical exercises and projects. More than just a collection of notebooks, it is a space where I can document what I learn, test different approaches and improve the way I work with data.

## What I'm Exploring

- **Python for Data:** Working with Python and libraries such as NumPy and Pandas.
- **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** Understanding, organizing and preparing datasets for analysis.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Investigating data, identifying patterns and exploring relationships.
- **Statistics:** Applying statistical concepts to better understand data.
- **Data Visualization:** Representing information and communicating insights through visualizations.
- **Data Science:** Exploring analytical methods and the foundations of predictive modeling.
